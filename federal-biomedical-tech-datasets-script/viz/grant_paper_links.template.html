Cited means the paper's PubMed record lists the grant, or NIH RePORTER lists the paper under the grant's core project number. Only papers about filoviruses are kept: a filovirus name in the title or MeSH headings, or twice in the abstract.
PI-authored papers match a grant PI by name and were published after the award started. They appear as rings and carry no topic color, since nothing ties them to this grant.
Topic pattern compares viruses (Ebola, Marburg, or filoviruses in general) and the nine filovirus proteins named in the grant's titles and abstracts across all fiscal years with those in the paper's title and abstract. Stays on topic: the paper names the same set or part of it. Adds: the paper names something the grant does not. No overlap: both name viruses or proteins, none shared. Conditions, symptoms and assay types are listed for each paper but left out of the pattern, because grants rarely write the disease name when they name the virus. Terms come from the KG's own vocabulary (kg_keywords.csv, kg_clinical_terms.csv).
Sources: NIH RePORTER projects and publications; PubMed. Built by build_entity_edges.py and build_links_page.py.
